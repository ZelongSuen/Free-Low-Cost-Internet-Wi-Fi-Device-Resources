Hardware: Windows 10/11 Pro + 1-Yr Warranty Hotspot: $15/mo Unlimited
PCs for People provides low-cost refurbished business-grade computers and affordable mobile internet to eligible low-income individuals. All operations from ConnectAll (InterConnection) have fully merged into this program.
Eligibility Requirements

To qualify, you must verify your identity and demonstrate low-income eligibility through one of the following pathways:

  • Government Assistance (Fastest): Pell Grant award letter for the current academic year; or approval documents for CalFresh / SNAP, Medi-Cal / Medicaid, SSI/SSDI, Section 8, or LIHEAP (issued within the past 12 months).
  • Income Verification: Household income at or below 200% of the Federal Poverty Guidelines (or local AMI limits). Accepted proof: most recent Federal Tax Return (Form 1040) or official unemployment benefits statement. (Pay stubs, W-2 forms, and pending FAFSA forms are not accepted).
What to Expect & Recommended Specs
  • Device Quality & Warranty: Fully wiped and refurbished enterprise-grade devices (e.g., Lenovo ThinkPad, Dell Latitude) running certified genuine Windows 10/11 Pro. Includes a 1-year limited hardware warranty.
  • Recommended Specs for School: Aim for at least 8GB RAM and 256GB SSD storage (Intel 8th Gen Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 3000 series and newer for full Windows 11 compatibility).
  • Low-Cost Mobile Hotspot: Unlimited mobile internet via T-Mobile nationwide coverage for $15/month after a one-time hotspot device purchase. Speeds may be subject to standard carrier prioritization during heavy network congestion.
How to Order
01
Prepare Documents: Have a digital copy of your photo ID and proof of eligibility ready (e.g., PDF of your Pell Grant letter).
02
Select & Checkout: Browse inventory at pcsrefurbished.com and submit your documents during online checkout.
03
Verification & Delivery: Verification takes 1–2 business days. Approved orders ship free via ground delivery within the continental U.S. (typically arriving in 4–7 business days).

California LifeLine is a state-administered initiative overseen by the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) designed to keep communication affordable by providing monthly discounts on essential home phone and mobile services for eligible California residents.
Key Program Benefits
  • Monthly Service Credits: Substantial ongoing bill reductions applied directly to qualifying home wireline or wireless phone plans.
  • No-Cost Mobile Plans & Free Device Options: Participating wireless providers often supply approved participants with a $0/month plan—featuring talk, text, data, and hotspot—paired with a basic smartphone.
  • One Connection Per Household: Designate your benefit toward either one landline or one wireless line per residence.
Eligibility Pathways
  • Pathway 1: Program-Based (Recommended): Active participation in CalFresh / SNAP, Medi-Cal / Medicaid, SSI, LIHEAP, Section 8, or Federal Pell Grants.
  • Pathway 2: Household Income Verification: Total annual household income falling within state-defined guidelines verified via tax returns or official benefit statements.

Assistive Technology & Accessibility Programs

State-mandated universal access equipment, amplified communication tools, and mobility phone accessories.

Free Adaptive Tech California Connect (CTAP - California Telephone Access Program) ↗

A state-mandated program by the CPUC providing free specialized telecommunications equipment and smartphone accessories to California residents with difficulty hearing, seeing, speaking, moving, or remembering. Includes amplified phones, speech-generating devices, and hands-free mobile phone accessories at zero cost.

Eligibility: No income requirements. Requires an application signed by a licensed physician, audiologist, or a campus SAS (Student Accessibility Services) counselor.
California Connect (administered under the California Telephone Access Program / CPUC) provides free specialized communication equipment and mobile accessories to California residents with communication accessibility needs.
Key Program Benefits
  • Specialized Adaptive Hardware at No Cost: Receive tailored tools—including amplified phones, high-contrast big-button devices, speech-generating tools, and hands-free smartphone accessories—at zero expense.
  • Universal Access (No Income Requirements): Funded by a state universal service surcharge; no household income limits or financial background checks.
  • Dedicated Setup Assistance: Access complimentary equipment setup guidance and technical support to ensure devices operate smoothly.
  • Direct Home Delivery: Approved equipment is shipped directly to your residence at zero shipping cost.
Eligibility Requirements
  • California Residency: Reside in the State of California and have access to basic telephone service (landline, mobile phone, or VOIP).
  • Professional Certification: Application certified by a licensed medical/accessibility professional (primary physician, audiologist, optometrist) or a campus Student Accessibility Services (SAS / DSPS) specialist.
Platform: caconnect.org Visit Official Portal ↗
